VAT note: Since you're not VAT registered, you pay VAT to suppliers but can't claim it back. Profit is calculated as: Sell Price − (Cost + VAT you pay). For example: £3.55 cost + £0.71 VAT (20%) = £4.26 total cost. If you sell for £18, your real profit is £13.74.
